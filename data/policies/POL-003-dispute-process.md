# POL-003 Dispute process

Effective January 1, 2026. How to dispute a debit card purchase you don't recognise or didn't authorise.

## Eligibility

You can dispute a purchase within 60 days of its posting date. Pending purchases can't be disputed until they post, usually within 3 business days. Only one dispute can be open per purchase: if one is already open, we give you its status instead of opening another.

## How to open a dispute

1. Tell us which purchase it is: the merchant and the amount.
2. Tell us what happened: you didn't make it, you were charged the wrong amount, you were charged twice, or you never received what you paid for.
3. If you didn't make the purchase, we recommend freezing your card (POL-002).

## What happens next

You receive a dispute reference number. Most disputes are resolved within 45 days, and we may contact you for more details. If the dispute is accepted, the amount is returned to your account. If it is rejected, we explain why. Dispute investigations are free (POL-001).

## Fraud

Fraud and stolen-card disputes are always passed to a Puffin Bank agent after they are opened.
