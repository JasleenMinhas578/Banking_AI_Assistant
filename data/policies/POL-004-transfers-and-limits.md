# POL-004 Transfers and limits

Effective January 1, 2026. Applies to transfers made through the Puffin Bank phone assistant.

## Who you can send to

Only between your own Puffin Bank accounts, for example chequing to savings. Payments to other people are not available through the assistant; a Puffin Bank agent can help with those.

## Limits

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Per-transfer limit | $2,500 |
| Daily limit | $5,000 in total per calendar day, Newfoundland time |
| Available balance | A transfer cannot take an account below $0 |
| Fee | $0 for transfers between your own accounts |

## Verification and confirmation

Transfers over $1,000 need a one-time code sent to your phone, in addition to your PIN. The code is valid for 5 minutes. Before any transfer, the assistant reads the amount and both accounts back to you and waits for a clear "yes".

## Timing and mistakes

Transfers between your own accounts are immediate. The assistant cannot reverse a completed transfer: you can move the money back yourself, or ask an agent for help. If a transfer is declined, the assistant tells you which rule applied, for example that the amount is over the daily limit.
