# POL-002 Card policy

Effective January 1, 2026. Covers lost, stolen and frozen Puffin Bank debit cards.

## Lost or stolen cards

- **Report right away.** If your card is lost or stolen, tell us as soon as you notice. The phone assistant can freeze it 24 hours a day.
- **Freezing is instant.** A frozen card is declined everywhere until it is unfrozen. Deposits and transfers between your own accounts keep working.
- **Suspicious purchases.** If you see a purchase you don't recognise, freeze the card first, then open a dispute (POL-003).

## Unfreezing a card

For your protection, only a Puffin Bank agent can unfreeze a card, after confirming your identity. The assistant cannot unfreeze a card.

## Replacement cards

Replacement cards arrive by mail in 5 to 7 business days. The first replacement each year is free; see POL-001 for fees after that.

## Zero liability

You are not responsible for purchases you did not authorise, as long as you report the card lost or stolen promptly and have not shared your PIN.

## Contactless limit

Tap payments are limited to $250 per purchase. Larger purchases need the chip and PIN.
