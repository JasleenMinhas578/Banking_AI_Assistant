# POL-001 Fee schedule

Effective January 1, 2026. Applies to all Puffin Bank personal accounts. All fees are in Canadian dollars.

## Account fees

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chequing monthly fee | $4.95 | Waived when the balance stays above $1,500 all month |
| Savings monthly fee | $0 | |
| Paper statements | $2.00 per month | Online statements are free |

## Transaction fees

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfers between your own accounts | $0 | |
| Non-sufficient funds (NSF) | $45.00 | Charged when a payment is declined for lack of funds |
| Foreign currency purchases | 2.5% of the purchase | Added to purchases made in a currency other than CAD |

## Card and dispute fees

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replacement debit card | $0 for the first per year, then $10 | No fee when the card was stolen or used fraudulently |
| Dispute investigation | $0 | |

## Overdraft

Puffin Bank accounts do not offer overdraft protection. A transfer or purchase that would take a balance below $0 is declined, and there is no overdraft fee because the payment never goes through.
