# POL-005 Security, privacy and using the assistant

Effective January 1, 2026. Frequently asked questions about the Puffin Bank phone assistant.

## What the assistant can do

Answer questions about our policies and fees, tell you your balances and recent purchases, freeze a lost or stolen card, open a dispute, and move money between your own accounts. For anything else, it connects you to an agent.

## How we verify you

With your 4-digit PIN, and a one-time code for transfers over $1,000. After 3 wrong PIN attempts, the session locks and we connect you to an agent. You stay verified for 10 minutes of inactivity; after that, we ask for your PIN again.

## What we will never ask for

Your full card number, your online banking password, or your PIN for anything other than verification on the keypad. If someone asks for these, it is not Puffin Bank. The assistant only ever says the last 4 digits of an account or card number.

## Who can use your account

Each customer must verify their own identity, even family members. The assistant cannot share or change another person's account details.

## What we keep

A masked record of each call for 30 days, with PINs and card numbers removed. The assistant may remember simple preferences, like your language, but never balances or transactions.

## Reaching a person

Say "agent" at any time. Agents are available 8 am to 8 pm Newfoundland time; the assistant is available 24 hours a day.
